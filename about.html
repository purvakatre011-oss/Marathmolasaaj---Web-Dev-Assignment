BEHIND THE CRAFT

Preserving Culture

Marathmola Saaj was born out of a deep affection for the timeless elegance of Maharashtrian heritage.

Every ornament—from our classic Ashtapailu Saaj and delicate AD Stone Naths to our handcrafted Earcuffs—is thoughtfully designed to celebrate traditional grace while staying lightweight and modern.

We take pride in keeping centuries-old royal jewellery traditions alive, bringing authentic charm to every special occasion and festive celebration.
